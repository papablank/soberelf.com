Day 23

The Mirror

The video for this day is coming soon.

We're good at taking other people's inventory. What they did wrong. Why they're the problem. We could write books about everybody else.

Eventually, recovery hands us a pencil and asks us to write about ourselves. That's terrifying, because we know what's in there. Or we think we do.

Here's what most of us found: it wasn't the horror story we expected. It was full of the same things everyone else had. Fear. Selfishness. Hurt. Not monsters. People. An honest look in the mirror isn't about beating ourselves up. It's about finally seeing clearly enough to change. We don't do it alone. We do it with a counselor or sponsor.
