Day 22

Shame

The video for this day is coming soon.

Guilt says, I did something bad. Shame says, I am something bad.

Shame is the one that kept us using. It told us we were too far gone, too damaged, too much to fix — so why bother trying? And shame loves the dark. It grows in secrets. It shrinks the moment we say it out loud to someone who doesn't flinch.

That's what the rooms are for. We tell the truth, and someone nods, because they did it too. We are not the worst thing we ever did. We're people with a disease, getting better. You can put the shame down. It was never yours to carry.
