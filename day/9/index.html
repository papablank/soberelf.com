Day 9

Hope

The video for this day is coming soon.

Hope is a dangerous word when it's lied to you a hundred times. This time will be different. This time I've got it handled.

So when someone says there's a power that can restore us to sanity, it's fair to squint. Here's the thing, though. We don't have to believe it all at once. We just have to look around the room. People who were worse off than us are laughing. Holding jobs. Raising kids. Something worked for them that never worked when they tried it alone. Call it the group. Call it whatever you want. We don't need a definition today. We just need to be willing to believe it might work for us too.
