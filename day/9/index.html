Clean For Today · Day 9 of 28

Hope

Every day is spoken by someone who has been in treatment for addiction. Their names stay private.

Hope is a hard word to trust when we've lied to ourselves with it a hundred times. This time will be different. This time I've got it handled.

So when someone says there's a power that can restore us to sanity, it's fair to be skeptical. Here's the thing, though. We don't have to believe it all at once. We just have to look around the room. People who were worse off than us are laughing. Holding jobs. Raising kids. Something worked for them that never worked when they tried it alone. Call it the group. Call it whatever you want. We don't need a definition. We just need to be willing to believe it might work for us too.
