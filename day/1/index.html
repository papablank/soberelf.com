Day 1

Gloves Down

The video for this day is coming soon.

Nobody walks into recovery proud. We get here beat — out of tricks, out of excuses, out of people who still believe us. And that's the good news.

Admitting we're powerless over our addiction isn't losing the fight. It's finally admitting the fight was rigged. For years we kept swinging at something that never got tired, never slept, never missed a chance. Today we get to set the gloves down.

We don't have to win. We just have to quit lying about who's been winning. That honesty feels like defeat at first. It isn't. It's the first solid ground we've stood on in a long time. Just for today, we don't have to beat it. We just have to tell the truth about it — and stay clean while we do.
