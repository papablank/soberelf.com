Clean For Today · Day 21 of 28

Progress

Every day is spoken by someone who has been in treatment for addiction. Their names stay private.

We wanted to be fixed by now. The anger gone, the patience perfect, the old habits erased. Then we snapped at someone in line at the store and decided we were doing recovery wrong.

Hold on. Not long ago, would we have even noticed? Would we have even apologized? Would we have stayed clean through it? We're not aiming for perfect. We're aiming for a little better than yesterday. And being honest about where we fell short. Progress is slow, quiet, and real. Look how far you've already walked.
