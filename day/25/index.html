Day 25

Earning It Back

The video for this day is coming soon.

We got clean and expected everyone to throw a parade in our honor. Instead, our family still checks our eyes. Still counts the money. Still flinches when the phone rings late.

That hurts. And it's fair. We spent years teaching them not to trust us. It's going to take more than a few good weeks to teach them something new. So we stop demanding trust and start earning it. Quietly. Consistently. Day after day, until our actions say what our words never could. Their healing runs on its own clock, not ours. Our job isn't to rush them. Our job is to keep showing up, clean.
