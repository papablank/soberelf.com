Day 5

Clean Isn't Numb

The video for this day is coming soon.

Nobody warned us about this part. We put the drugs down, and the feelings showed up all at once, like they'd been waiting in the parking lot for years.

Anger with nowhere to go. Grief we skipped. Joy so sharp it almost hurts. For a long time we kept all of it at arm's length. Now there's no arm.

Here's what the people with time will tell us: feelings aren't facts, and they aren't emergencies. They pass. Every single one of them passes, if we don't use over it. But if a feeling includes wanting to die or hurt yourself, that is an emergency. Tell someone now, or call or text 988. This is what being alive feels like — loud, messy, and ours. We're not broken because it hurts. We're healing because we can finally feel it.
