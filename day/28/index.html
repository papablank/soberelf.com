Clean For Today · Day 28 of 28

Pass It On

Every day is spoken by someone who has been in treatment for addiction. Their names stay private.

Somebody did this for us. Somebody told the truth in a room we almost didn't walk into.

For a while, we sat in the back and said nothing. Listening felt like enough. It wasn't. When we keep quiet, nobody knows what we're carrying, and nobody can help us carry it.

There's an old saying in recovery: we can't keep what we have unless we give it away. Here's what it means. Recovery we keep to ourselves starts to fade. Recovery we say out loud stays real. So raise your hand. Not a speech. Just a quick check-in: here's where I'm at today.

It isn't for them. It's for us.
