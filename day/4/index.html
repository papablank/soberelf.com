Clean For Today · Day 4 of 28

The Heaviest Phone

Every day is spoken by someone who has been in treatment for addiction. Their names stay private.

There's a phone in your pocket that weighs about six ounces. Except when we need to call our counselor or sponsor. Then it weighs a thousand pounds.

Funny how that works. We'd text the dealer at three in the morning without a second thought. But asking for help feels like lifting a truck. That's not weakness talking. That's the disease of addiction, protecting itself. It knows a phone call can kill it.

So we lift it anyway. We don't need the right words. “Hey, it's me, I'm having a day” is a whole sentence. Someone on the other end has been exactly where we are, and they're waiting for that call more than we know. We pick it up. It gets lighter every time.
