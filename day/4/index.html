Day 4

The Heaviest Phone

The video for this day is coming soon.

There's a phone in your pocket that weighs about six ounces. Except when we need to call our counselor or sponsor. Then it weighs a thousand pounds.

Funny how that works. We'd text a dealer at three in the morning without a second thought, but asking for help feels like lifting a truck. That's not weakness talking. That's the disease, protecting itself. It knows a phone call can kill it.

So we lift it anyway. We don't need the right words. "Hey, it's me, I'm having a day" is a whole sentence. Someone on the other end has been exactly where we are, and they're waiting for that call more than we know. Pick it up. It gets lighter every time.
