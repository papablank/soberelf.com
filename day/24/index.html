DAY 24

The Plan

day 24 of 28Clean For Today

Every day is spoken by someone who has been in treatment for addiction. Their names stay private.

Discharge day feels like the finish line, but it isn't. It's the starting line, and the first few days out are when a lot of us go back. Not because we wanted to. Because we walked out with no plan, and our old lives had a plan waiting for us.

So let's create a plan before we leave. Where are we sleeping the first night out? Who's picking us up? Where's the first meeting, and what time does it start? Whose numbers are in our phone? When is the first appointment with your counselor?

Write it down. Go over it with your counselor or sponsor. A plan won't make you feel ready. It doesn't have to. It just has to be there when the feeling isn't. Today, we start writing our plan.
