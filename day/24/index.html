Day 24

The Plan

The video for this day is coming soon.

The first days after treatment feel like a finish line. They aren't. They're the starting line, and they're when a lot of us went back. Not because we wanted to. Because we walked out with no plan, and the old life had one waiting for us.

So we build ours ahead of time. Where are we sleeping tonight? Who's picking us up? Where's the next meeting, and what time does it start? Whose numbers are in the phone? When's the next appointment?

Write it down. Go over it with a counselor or sponsor. A plan won't make us feel ready. It doesn't have to. It just has to be there when the feeling isn't. Today, we start writing ours.
