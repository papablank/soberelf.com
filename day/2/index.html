Day 2

Forever Is Too Heavy

The video for this day is coming soon.

The disease of addiction loves big numbers. Never again. The rest of your life. Forever. The disease hands us forever like a weight that's too heavy to lift, and laughs at us when we drop it.

We don't carry forever. We carry today. Today has edges. It has a morning and a night and a pillow at the end. We can stay clean from here to that pillow. Nobody in the rooms got years of clean time by staring at years. They got them the same way we get this one: one day stacked against another. Most of those days were ordinary. Some of them were ugly. Every one of them was enough.

So put forever down. Nobody can lift it. We pick up today. It fits in our hands.
