Day 11

Just One

Every day is spoken by someone who has been in treatment for addiction. Their names stay private.

It usually shows up as a whisper. Just one. Just this weekend. Just to take the edge off.

The disease of addiction is patient. It doesn't need us to use today. It just needs us to keep a little door open in the back of our minds. We call it a reservation, a someday, a special occasion. That door is how a lot of us went back to the bottom.

So we close it out loud. We tell someone about the whisper the moment we hear it. Secrets keep it alive. Daylight takes it away. There is no “just one” for us. There's just today. And today, we stay clean.
