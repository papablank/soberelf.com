Day 6

Small Things

The video for this day is coming soon.

Recovery doesn't usually look like a movie. No swelling strings, no sunrise on a mountaintop. Mostly it looks like a made bed. A paycheck that goes where it's supposed to. A promise kept on a Tuesday when nobody's watching.

We used to chase big. Big highs, big plans, big comebacks we never came back from. Now the miracle is small and quiet, and it shows up every day. Showing up on time. Answering the phone. Looking someone in the eye.

Don't skip the small things. They're not the warm-up to recovery. They are recovery. Stack enough of them together, and one day you look up and realize you've built a life.
