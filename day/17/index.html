Day 17

Fear

The video for this day is coming soon.

Fear has been driving a lot longer than we've been clean. Fear of being found out. Fear of being alone. Fear that this is all we'll ever be. For years we used to keep it quiet. Now it's loud again, and nothing is muffling it.

Here's what we learn: fear is a feeling, not a forecast. It tells us a lot about our past and almost nothing true about tomorrow. Courage isn't the absence of fear. It's doing the next right thing with our hands still shaking. We don't need to beat fear today. We just keep it in the passenger seat, not behind the wheel.
