Day 7

Still Here

The video for this day is coming soon.

If you're hearing this, you're still here. Don't rush past that.

Some of us didn't make it. We carry their names. And every morning we wake up clean, we're doing something they didn't get the chance to do. That's not guilt. That's purpose.

And if yesterday went sideways — if you used, if you ran, if you're watching this from the bottom again — the door didn't lock behind you. It never does. Nobody in the rooms is keeping score. We're just glad you came back.

Today is new. It doesn't know what you did. It only knows you're here. So let's start there. Together.
