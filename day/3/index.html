Day 3

The Chair

Every day is spoken by someone who has been in treatment for addiction. Their names stay private.

Somewhere tonight there's a folding chair with nobody in it yet. A warm cup of coffee. A few friendly faces. A room that smells like second chances.

We used to think we were too smart for rooms like that. Too different. Too far gone. Or not far gone enough. Then we sat down, and somebody said something we recognize. It was our story in a stranger's mouth.

That's the miracle nobody advertises. Not the speeches. The recognition. The moment we realized we were never as alone as the disease of addiction told us. The chair is still there. It doesn't care what we did. It only asks that we sit down and keep coming back.
