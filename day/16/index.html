Clean For Today · Day 16 of 28

The Tape

Every day is spoken by someone who has been in treatment for addiction. Their names stay private.

We replay it. What they said, what they did, what we should have said back. We've run the tape so many times we know every word. Resentment feels like justice. It feels like keeping score for the good guys.

But the person we're angry at usually isn't losing any sleep. We are. We're the ones swallowing the poison and waiting for them to get sick.

So try something different. Play the tape forward. Where does this one end if we keep running it? For a lot of us, it ends with using. We don't have to pretend it didn't happen. We just have to look at what our part was, and what we're willing to set down. And if what happened to us was abuse, there's no part to find. That one was never ours. Either way, we don't carry it alone. Talk it through with a counselor or sponsor. Write it out. Let it out of the dark. Freedom is worth more than being right.
