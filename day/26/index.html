Day 26

Saturday Night

The video for this day is coming soon.

Nobody warned us about Saturday nights. The work week ends, the phone goes quiet, and suddenly there's a whole evening with nothing in it.

Boredom used to be our cue. The disease of addiction likes to remind us the old life was never boring. That's a lie. It was the same people, the same places, the same night over and over.

So we plan the weekend like it matters, because it does. A meeting, then coffee with whoever's still in the parking lot. A movie. A long ride. The thing we always said we'd learn. Clean fun is a skill, and like any skill, it's awkward at first. Then one night we realize we laughed until midnight and never once thought about using.
