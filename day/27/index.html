Day 27

Grateful Anyway

The video for this day is coming soon.

Some days are just hard. The car won't start. The rent is late. Somebody we love won't call back.

And on those days, someone at the meeting says they're grateful, and we want to tell them exactly where they can shove their gratitude. But gratitude isn't pretending the bad stuff isn't happening. It's noticing what's still standing in the middle of it. A bed. A phone with a counselor's or sponsor's number in it. A day where the worst thing that happened was bad, and we didn't make it worse. Write three things down tonight. Small ones count. Especially small ones. Gratitude on a bad day is the strongest kind.
