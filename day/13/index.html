Day 13

Open Mind

The video for this day is coming soon.

We came in knowing everything. We knew what would work, what wouldn't, and why the people in the rooms were different from us.

Then we looked around. A room full of people who've been exactly where we are, and who want us to make it. Plenty of people who need that never find it. Maybe it's time to let somebody else's experience in. An open mind doesn't mean we believe everything. It means we stop arguing long enough to listen. Some of us say clean. Some of us say sober. It's the same road.

And if you take medication for addiction as prescribed, that's clean time too. We try the suggestion before we decide it won't work. We let the old-timer finish the sentence. Some of the best things in recovery came wrapped in advice we almost ignored.
