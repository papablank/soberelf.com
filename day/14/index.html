Clean For Today · Day 14 of 28

Willing

Every day is spoken by someone who has been in treatment for addiction. Their names stay private.

We don't have to be ready. We don't have to be sure. Most of us weren't.

What we needed was much smaller than that. A little willingness. A crack in the door. Enough to show up one more time, make one more call, try one more thing we swore we'd never do. Willingness isn't a feeling. It's an action. We do it scared. We do it tired. We do it while the voice in our head says this won't work. And somewhere in the doing, the feeling catches up.

Enough showing up starts to look a lot like faith from the outside. It is.
