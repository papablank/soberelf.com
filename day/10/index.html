Clean For Today · Day 10 of 28

Letting Go

Every day is spoken by someone who has been in treatment for addiction. Their names stay private.

We are experts at holding on. Holding grudges. Holding secrets. Holding the steering wheel with both fists while the car goes straight into the ditch. Letting go sounds like giving up. It feels like falling.

But look at what our grip has actually gotten us.

Letting go doesn't mean we stop caring or stop trying. It means we stop running the whole show on our own broken judgment. We make a decision, and then we make it again tomorrow, and the day after that. Our hands were never built to hold on to this much. Open them. See what lands.
