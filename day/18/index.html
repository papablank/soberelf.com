Day 18

The Coffee

The video for this day is coming soon.

Somebody had to make the coffee. Somebody unlocked the door, set out the chairs, and stayed late to stack them again.

For a long time, we never wondered who that somebody was. Then one night, someone handed us the keys. Service sounds like a big word, but it's mostly small things. Greeting the newcomer. Washing the pot. Giving someone a ride. And here's what nobody tells you: it's not really for them. When we're busy being useful, there's less room for the self-obsession that kept us sick. Grab a job at your home group. Any job. It's the easiest way to stay.
