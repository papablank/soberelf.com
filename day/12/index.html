Day 12

It Passes

The video for this day is coming soon.

A craving feels like it will last forever. It feels like a wall we can't get around, a noise that won't stop until we feed it. Our disease lies about that, too.

Cravings rise, crest, and fall — every single one, as long as we don't use over it. So we ride it out. We call someone. We eat something. We go to a meeting, take a walk, take a shower, pray if we pray. We do anything except the one thing. And an hour later, we notice it's gone, and we're still standing. That's a win. Count it.
