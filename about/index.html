About

"I've sat where you're sitting. Six times."

Dave B.

"It took me six inpatient rehabs, two overdoses that almost killed me, and a prison sentence. The first five times, I thought I was the exception. Three of those times, I walked out before it was over. My addiction had a story for every occasion, and I believed every one. The sixth time, I came in from a tent along the highway in Oregon. I'd lost custody of my son, my marriage, my career, and every dollar I'd ever made. The rehab didn't change the sixth time. I did. I stopped doing it my way, and I let people help me."

From the foreword to Clean For Today

What we stand for

Honest

Open-minded
