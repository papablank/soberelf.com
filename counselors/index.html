How it works on the unit

One page a day, about two minutes to read, no phone needed. The days can stand alone, so a reader can start anywhere, skip ahead, or take the rest home.

Where it stands

No licensed clinician has reviewed it yet. That's why it comes to you first.

"I'll make every change you ask for. If you'd cut a page, it's cut."

How a pilot works

"Nothing happens without your OK, and you can stop it at any step."

  1. "You review the proof, on whatever timeline works for you."
  2. "I make your changes and bring back a clean copy."
  3. "The center gives written permission, from whoever needs to approve it. A short letter is enough, and it protects you."
  4. "I print 10–30 copies at no cost to the center, with your readmission number on the walk-out page."
  5. "You hand them out to whoever you think it would help."
  6. "Feedback stays anonymous. An optional tear-out card on the last page asks two questions: 'Which day helped most?' and 'Which page did you read twice?' Staff collect the cards. There are no fields for names, dates or initials."
  7. "We talk afterward and decide together whether to keep going, change it, or stop."
