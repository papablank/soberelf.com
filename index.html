Clean For Today: Your First 28 Days

Something to read when the unit goes quiet.

A pocket booklet of 28 one-page reflections for people in inpatient treatment. It works without a phone or a screen, and each day is being made into a short animated video so it keeps working after discharge.

Built for the unit

One page a day. No screen needed.

  • No phone or screen needed. One page a day, about two minutes to read.
  • The "Before You Walk Out" page asks the reader to wait an hour and tell a person before they leave.
  • Each day is read by a different person in recovery. Day 1 is recorded, and ten more readers have agreed to record.
