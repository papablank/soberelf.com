28 short readings for your first days clean. Each one is spoken by a person who has been in treatment for addiction. We keep their names private. You can still hear them.

About a minute. Music by Aaron Scharmer.

Why their voices

Counselors, nurses, doctors and judges all want you to make it. But on night two, with your bag half packed, the voice that gets through is someone who sat in that same chair.

"I've sat in that chair six times. The first five times, I thought I was the exception. Three of those times, I walked out before it was over."

Dave B.

How it works

One day at a time. Start anywhere.

  • Hear the day, then read it. About two minutes.
  • Each day stands alone. Skip ahead, go back, take the rest home.
  • The pocket booklet works without a phone or a screen. Its Before You Walk Out page is for the night you want to leave.
